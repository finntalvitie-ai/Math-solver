Math Solver

Type or write an equation, inequality, system or word problem. Get the answer and every step that leads to it.

Any letter can be the unknown. Use < > ≤ ≥ for inequalities, put each equation of a system on its own line (or separate them with “;”), and start with “solve for r:” to rearrange a formula. Supports + − × ÷ ^, brackets, √, sin, cos, tan (radians), ln, log, abs, !, %, π and e. On iPad, write straight into the box with Apple Pencil — Scribble turns your handwriting into text. Shift + Enter adds a new line.
